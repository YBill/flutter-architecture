CHAPTER HUNDRED AND NINE

CAMILLE'S POV :

Being the new Alpha of the pack I had taken to my newfound role with ease. So far I was running things smoothly and confidently in the pack, and all the wolves were responding positively to my leadership and Liam has also been of great help and service to me.

At first there had been some hesitation and resistance, a very great one but now it seemed to have reduced. Though the Pack was now getting used to me but some of them didn't accept me. But I was determined to prove myelf, and soon enough I will win the respect of all my pack mates.

I had taken on the responsibility of leading the pack with enthusiasm and energy, and soon enough things had started to run smoothly. I had set up laws and regulations to make sure that everyone was obeying the rules, and I was quick to punish anyone who broke them, sometimes I would even give a warning or talk to the person.

I had also started to teach the younger wolves about the importance of working together and respecting each other, as well as teaching them the values of loyalty and integrity.

I had also started to take on more responsibilities outside of the pack,like our pack allies, such as helping the other packs in the area with their problems and issues, and supporting them in their endeavors. I had also taken it upon myself to represent the pack in negotiations with other Alphas.

I was proud of how well I had been doing as the new Alpha of the pack, and I was looking forward to continuing to lead them in the right direction.

Today had been a great day, work filled but still interesting. Now it was time for dinner and I , my aunt and Aiden's mother was on our sits eating.

Suddenly a man and his wife came into the house and straight to were we where having dinner.

"You! You are the one that killed our daughter. Look at yourself, power seeking wretch", the woman shouted angrily at me. I couldn't even understand who they were or who they were talking about.

I still sat down in my sit and stared at them. Aiden's mother was about to say something but I already beat her to it before she could talk.

"I'm sorry but who might your daughter be?", I asked the couple, trying to be as calm as possible.

"Oh you don't know, your Luna that you killed. Luna Laila, she was our daughter and you killed her like she didn't matter", the man spat out with disgust as he looked at me like he would kill me.

I scoffed and then laughed, "of course Laila doesn't matter. She's not an important person, so I don't see why you will come into my house, ranting and making a fuse".

"How dare you be so disrespectful? You have no shame ", Laila's mother said to me.

"I have no shame? Look at the both of you, coming here to rant, instead of you to go mourn your daughter. She wasn't even fit to be Luna, she wasn't even the real Luna and your here condemning me like your daughter Laila is some kind of saint. If you have nothing better to say, you can leave and if your tired of the pack, your free to go", I stated, leaving no room for arguments.

"Do you know what? I and my wife will leave this stupid pack", Laila's dad said as he held his wife hands to leave.

"Next time watch your mouth, I won't be so loving", I said dangerously, turning to look at them and then smiling. The couple's left my house and I'm guessing they would leave the pack too.

Aiden's mother then looked at me as if she was trying to understand something.

I raised my brows at her, asking, "do you have any problem with what just happened?".

"No I don't, anyways, let's enjoy the dinner please", Aiden's mother said, my aunt also agreeing. Aiden's mother has been trying to make me forgive her and her family for everything they did to me and my own family but I just wasn't ready yet. My trust wasn't strong enough for them.

After dinner was done, I and my aunt saw a movie together before heading to bed. Laying down on the bed in my room, flashes of Aiden's face came to me. I was feeling so restless, even my wolf. I stood up and walked to my wardrobe and found one of Aiden's hoodies. I quickly put the hoodie on and went back to bed, his scent on it.

It didn't take long, I felt calm and slept off.


Next morning >>>>>>>

I woke feeling a little groggy, but still with a sense of anticipation. I quickly got ready, freshening up in the bathroom and putting on a simple dress, before going downstairs to have breakfast. Surprised, I finished my breakfast and headed to the office. When I arrived, I was even more surprised to find Alpha Luke there.

"Alpha Luke!", I smiled widely giving him a short hug as he stood up and hugged me back .

"Please sit sit", I told him going over to take my own sit behind my desk.

Liam was actually standing beside the door, I didn't mind though.

I looked at Alpha Luke asking him about everyone, his mate and the twins. I was happy and glad to hear that they were all doing well.

"So what brings you here, is there anything I could help you with?", I asked him, wanting him to feel very welcomed.

"No Camille", I noticed he didn't use my Alpha title but I let it go, doesn't really matter.

"I came to talk to you about our two packs", he stated.

I looked at him, getting confused, "And what about our two packs?".
