His parents where still alive and strong, enjoying their time together with each other. Over the years Marcellus had removed the thought of having a mate or even getting one from his mind. Liam and Charlotte's where officially mate and now mated and happy, Marcellus didn't feel like he was lacking out because he didn't have a mate. He was still strong on his own and besides that he still ran the Pack very well.

His mum, Camille had urged him to find someone he liked, maybe mingle or he can still go in search of his mate and get back together, solve everything together and be happy but that was a discussion that Marcellus didn't want to have.

"Mum please, can we not start. I don't have a mate and I never did", he said firmly that day as they all had breakfast.

His mother and father looked at him. Marcellus may look happy and contented but he was missing something and he wouldn't admit it but they knew. They knew he was hurt but he should have let go by now, years had passed and not for once had they seen him bring a girl back home.

His mum, Camille and even his Dad was starting to get worried, Marcellus had some trust issues which they understood but the way he was going about everything made them worried. They just wanted him to be happy and they knew he missed her but he locked that part away and threw the key out forever.

Today now at the Phoenix Pack, they had a visitor, a beautiful young lady and the maids had informed Luna Camille about the visitor. She came down to see who it was.

Luna Camille returned the greeting also with a smile as she asked the lady to take a sit and had a sit herself as well.

"So why have you come here and what's your name?", Luna Camille questioned looking at the lady in her front.

"My name is Morgana Le Fay and I'm here to see Marcellus", the lady said softly. Luna Camille gasped at this, she was both happy to finally meet Marcellus mate, he had told them her name when the discussion was out and all. But Luna Camille was also worried on how everything will play out. Marcellus might not like the idea of Morgana being here but what could go wrong?.

His mum tried calling him back both he ignored her and even Morgana ran after him and grabbed his arm but he swatted it away and got into his ride, heading for his meeting.

All the feelings cane rushing back, everything that had happened, the joy, happiness, love, betrayal and hurt came back to him. Why did she even come here, how did she even know the location?
