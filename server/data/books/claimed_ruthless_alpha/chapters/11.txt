"Here" he tossed a blanket towards me. With sleepy eyes I took it and muttered thank you.

"But who gave this to me?" I asked, my curiosity getting the best of me.

Aiden? To say I was shocked was an understatement. He hasn't even come to see me since him and his Godforsaken Luna put me in here and now he gives me blankets? Something inside me was a bit happy though that he did.
