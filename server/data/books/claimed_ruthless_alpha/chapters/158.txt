As they pushed the gate open, the first thing that striked Marcellus was the peaceful stillness of the forest. The only sounds he could hear were the gentle rustling of leaves and the occasional chirping of birds.

In another room, there's a large table covered in crystals, candles, and other magical tools. This is where they gather to perform rituals and spells, harnessing the power of the natural world to manifest our intentions, Marcellus supposed.

There was also a training area, where our younger witches can practice their powers and hone their skills. Here, they learn how to harness their energy and control their magic, as well as how to defend themselves against any potential threats.

Throughout the coven, there's a sense of camaraderie and support. It was a tight-knit community, and it looked like they all worked together to help each other grow and develop our abilities.

As Marcellus took a deep breath and settled into the welcoming atmosphere of the coven, he enjoyed everything he saw and understood.
