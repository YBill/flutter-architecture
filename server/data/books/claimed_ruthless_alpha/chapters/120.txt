Meanwhile......

Alpha Luke, set out on a journey to a distant land in search of a witch said to reside deep in the heart of a dark forest. He had heard rumors of her abilities and sought her out in the hopes of gaining knowledge and power for himself and his pack.

As he traveled through the thick and tangled woods, the Alpha could feel the weight of the forest pressing in on him. The trees seemed to loom overhead, casting deep shadows on the forest floor. The only sounds were the rustling of leaves and the occasional hoot of an owl.

The Alpha pressed on, determined to find the witch no matter what. He had heard stories of her fearsome powers, but he was not afraid. He was the Alpha, and he would not be deterred by mere rumors.

Finally, he reached a clearing in the forest. In the center of the clearing stood a small cottage, smoke curling from its chimney. The Alpha approached the cottage cautiously, his senses on high alert. He could feel the power emanating from the cottage, and he knew that he had found the witch.

He stepped up to the door and knocked. There was no answer. The Alpha pushed the door open and stepped inside. The cottage was small and dimly lit, but he could see a figure sitting in a rocking chair by the fireplace. The figure was an old woman, her hair white as snow and her eyes black as coal.

The Alpha stepped forward and spoke. "I have come to see the witch of the woods," he said. "I have heard of your powers and I seek your knowledge and guidance."

The old woman looked up at the Alpha and smiled. "Welcome, Alpha," she said. "I have been expecting you. I know why you have come, and I will share my knowledge with you. But you must be willing to pay the price."

The Alpha knew that there would be a price for the witch's knowledge, but he was willing to pay whatever it took to gain the power he sought. He agreed to the witch's terms and she began to teach him the ways of magic and the secrets of the forest.

As the days passed, the Alpha learned more and more from the witch. He grew stronger and more powerful. He had informed his family that he wouldbe coming back in a few days, so no one bothered to look for him as he was doing well.

Alpha Luke knew that it would be difficult to win the Two Moon's Pack, so he decided to go get some powers to make him stronger, faster and powerful.
