Nara led the other witches through the winding path through the woods as soon as they landed in Salem Massachusetts. The moon shone brightly in the night sky, providing a faint light to guide them. They all followed her in silence, their eyes glued to the back of her cloak as she made her way through the undergrowth.

Nara stopped and held up her hand, signaling for the others to be still. She sniffed the air and then pointed to the left.

"This way," she said, her voice low and mysterious.

The other witches followed her, the dense forest becoming thicker and more foreboding the further they went. Finally, they reached the edge of a clearing, and Nara pointed ahead, siting the House Of The Seven Reds building.

"The place we are looking for is just ahead," Nara said.

The Tansa Leader was already scared and started pleading with Nara to let her go but Nara was in no mood to hear or have mercy. Nara made the Tansa leader kneel before her and slowly Nara made the Tansa Leader suffer. She use her mind to make the Tansa leader burn from inside.
