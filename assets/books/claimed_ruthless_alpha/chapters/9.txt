CHAPTER NINE

Meanwhile on the other hand , some years back before Aiden's Dad died. Camille's Aunt had left the pack and was looking for a way to get her niece back, something must be wrong since they wouldn't let her see Camille. She was supposed to be there and protect her little niece but she couldn't.



On leaving the Two Moons Pack, she cut all ties with them and was going to come back one day for her niece. Just In case her niece would Come by the house if she can , she left some signs to where she would be.

There were secret signs used to send messages in their family earlier, Camille was taught how to read and understand it, such a fast learner and clever. She arranged everything and put the note in a secrete place where only Camille can get a hold of it, everything was done well so Camille wouldn't miss a spot.

After that was done , she took her things and left secretly. She was sure no one would look for her now. She was on a Journey to the Red Sky Pack, she had a friend there and she decided to join them as a new Pack member.

The Two Moons Pack had brought her so much pain and suffering over the past years. They didn't even give her two family that died at battle a good burial. She didn't know that they could behave like this.

On reaching the Red Sky Pack, she was stopped at the border. Of course they wouldn't just let her pass.

"What are you doing on another's Land?" she was asked. She had already changed back to her human form and worn her clothes knowing she would be questioned.

"I traveled a long way and I'm here to see the Luna" she replied back.

"What business do you have with the Luna" the guard gave a snarl .
"You smell like a rogue".

"I'm not a rogue, I left my pack. I wasn't casted out, they had done so many wrong things and I couldn't stay there anymore. Please tell the Luna that I'm here to see her, tell her it's Zara her old friend" she explained and asked politely. The guard could tell I was being sincere and told to wait a bit.


After a while of waiting the other guards there allowed me pass. One followed her and showed her the way to the Alpha's home.

Getting there she already saw her , Luna Abigail, her friend she hasn't seen for years. They rushed to each other and hugged as tears dropped from their eyes.

"I've missed you Zara, how are you? Your niece and everyone doing. I stopped hearing from you and I thought something had happened to you, we couldn't come to your pack because of the issues between my pack and yours". Abigail asked.

"I've missed you a lot too Abigail and That is no Pack of mine , I'll explain everything once I settle down. Because I have come to become part of the Red Sky Pack". She had a surprised looked on her face, I knew she has so many questions and I'll give her the answers she needed.

She lead me inside and introduced me to her mate, Alpha Luke,the Alpha of the Red Sky Pack. The Red Sky Pack was one of the strongest and biggest Pack in  the world, they had so many things and equipments one could think of.
I gave a short bow as I greeted.
The Alpha was kind enough to welcome me with a smile.

"So you're the one who has made my Luna so excited, that she basically ran to see you. I'm delighted to have you here, she has talked about you so much and I thank you for keeping her safe till I met here" he said and I smiled back.

"I wouldn't regret what I did for her, she was such a sweet soul".

I remembered when I first met Abigail, she was liked by a  crazy Alpha , he abuses girls and use them as slaves for his pleasure. They had known since they were little and when the Alpha had set eyes on Abigail, he wanted her at all cost but I couldn't accept, her family couldn't help her so I took it upon myself to do so.


I had heard things he did to those poor innocent girls and I won't let it happen to my friend so I fought for her, I almost got killed but my father came and killed the Alpha. He was also a warrior and from that day we had each other's back.

"Sweetheart Zara would love to join our pack, I'm sure I told you she's from the Two Moons Pack" Abigail told her mate.

"Your welcome to join the Pack Zara . But won't your Pack come looking for you, you know we aren't in good terms with them" he asked wondering.

"I'll explain everything to you both" I told them as we all sat down. The maids were asked to take my bags and keep them in the room for me, for which I was grateful. I told them everything that had happened over these years, some things brought tears to my eyes . When I was done there was so much silence.

"But why would they do such. She's your niece" Abigail exploded, she was so furious on how they had treated me.

"That's the problem Abi, if nothing was wrong, who won't they let me see her. I have been so worried, there was no way to get in and I don't know what to do. I would need your help getting her at all cost" I pleaded with them, I don't even know if she's alive and well or not .

"We want to help you but right now a war is coming up between the Two Moons Pack and ours, we can't make any irrational moves but when the time is right we will get her, just exercise patience and relax we will find a way".


Alpha Luke assured me.
I was very grateful that at least they were willing to help. After the whole talk, I and Abigail went a way, we talked about the things we had missed in each other's life.

I found out that they had two kids , a boy and a girl for that matter, but they weren't home now and I would meet them later, I was so glad.

Zara had lost her mate earlier than she thought and hadn't gotten with anyone ever since.
