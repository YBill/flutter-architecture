"Don't worry you will meet them soon and be properly introduced", Lord Dimitri told him as he patted Charles' back.

His brother emerged from his room soon after, looking just as dashing. He had taken a shower, shaved and wore a fitted navy blue suit with a white shirt and black tie. He looked as if he had stepped straight out of a fashion magazine. His military training had taught him how to dress sharp.

Together they made their way down to the feast, which was already in full swing. The hall was decorated with banners and tapestries, and long tables were laden with all sorts of delicious foods and drinks.

As they made their way to their seats, Lord Dimitri's brother couldn't help but stare in awe at the splendor of the hall. He had been to many impressive places during his time in the military, but he had never seen anything quite like this.

The feast was a lavish affair, with entertainment provided by musicians and jesters. There was roast beef, mutton, and chicken, along with a variety of vegetables, bread, and cheeses. The wine flowed freely, and the guests were in high spirits.

Soon Marcellus, his mate, his parents/ family and few pack members arrived at the party.

As the werewolves arrived, Lord Dimitri greeted them warmly, embracing them as allies. The werewolves, too, were pleased to see Lord Dimitri, having formed a bond with him during their battles together against the common enemy.

As they approached, Lord Dimitri introduced them to his brother, who stood with a false smile on his face. The werewolves could sense something was off about him, as he seemed hesitant and guarded around them.

Lord Dimitri noticed the tension and attempted to diffuse the situation. "Brother, these are our allies. The werewolves have fought alongside us and helped us in our battle against the enemy. They are trustworthy and have proven their loyalty time and time again."

His brother nodded politely, but his smile never reached his eyes. The werewolves exchanged glances, unsure of how to proceed, though they were on guard. Lord Dimitri's brother was behaving a bit weird to them.

Lord Dimitri sensed the tension and coldness and stepped in to mediate. "Perhaps we should all sit down and discuss our next course of action. We have much to plan and the werewolves have valuable insights to offer."

The group moved towards a large table in the center of the room, and Lord Dimitri motioned for the werewolves to take a seat. His brother hesitated for a moment before reluctantly joining them.

The discussion began, and Lord Dimitri made sure to include the werewolves in every decision. His brother, however, remained aloof and distant, clearly not wanting to engage with their new allies.

Despite his brother's reluctance, Lord Dimitri knew that the werewolves were essential to their success. He made it clear that they were to be treated with the utmost respect and that their opinions would be valued.

Still, the night went on with everyone enjoying themselves, there was dance battles and other classic dance, food to enjoy and many interesting things to do.

The werewolves simply enjoyed themselves even though Lord Dimitri's brother was not welcoming but they didn't think much into it, maybe he was just not comfortable yet or something.
